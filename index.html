FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. If you can plug in a USB drive and double click a file, you can use this.

Is it really as good as ChatGPT?

It runs the same class of open source models used across the AI industry. For everyday writing, questions, brainstorming and document chat, it holds its own. It will not browse the web, because nothing on it touches the internet. That is the point.

What computer do I need?

Any Windows or Mac from the last several years. 6GB of RAM for the Starter models, 8GB recommended for Standard and Pro.

How fast is it?

Answers take about 10 to 30 seconds depending on your computer. It runs on your processor, not a giant data center, so it trades a little speed for total privacy.

Can I get new models later?

Yes. The drive includes a built in installer. Run it, pick from the menu, or paste a link to any compatible model from HuggingFace. New models download to the drive and everything keeps running offline after that.

I moved the drive to a new computer and got an error.

Close the error and double click the launcher again. It clears the old computer's cached settings automatically and starts fresh.

What if it does not work for me?

Email us within 30 days for a full refund. No hoops.
